Common Health Issues

This is a list of some of the health conditions that Aussies are prone to.

  • Autoimmune Thyroiditis
  • Hemangiosarcoma
  • Lymphoma
  • Cataracts
  • Elbow Dysplasia
  • Epilepsy
  • Hip Dysplasia
  • Multi-Drug Resistance 1 (MDR1)
  • Moderate to Severe Allergies
